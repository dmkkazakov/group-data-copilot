## Verification

- 48 research unittest methods pass, including real MCP initialization, case exchange, partial restart, full restart, invalid messages and stalled-server handling.
- 17 separate CSV-application tests pass. The installed console application runs on the supplied synthetic example.
- The mutation experiment changes one of 13 fields in each of 40 assertions: 520 corruptions. The full comparison rejects all 520, and accepts all 40 clean controls. The deliberately weak source-field-presence baseline accepts both. This is not a measured extraction-accuracy rate.
- `verify_release.py` checks recomputed outputs, the checkpoint, mutation results, the exact submission abstract, all 16 bilingual equations and key manuscript numbers against the corpus. The manifest checks released file bytes.
- The five downloaded issuer PDFs were fetched again; every SHA-256 digest matches the initial source copy. See `data/source_recheck.json`.
- Primary pages were reinspected for Berkshire's class counts and reported percentages, Tencent's attributed holdings and contract structure, HSBC's dated shareholder notices and Singtel's treasury-adjusted denominator and management date.
- Both revised Word documents are rendered to PDF and reviewed page by page before release. Original issuer reports are not redistributed.

## Unresolved and bounded claims

Berkshire's disclosed inputs still give 14.348232% economic participation versus the reported 14.4%. The difference is 0.051768 percentage points and cannot be removed by direct one-decimal rounding. Both values remain; no issuer error or undocumented explanation is asserted.

The MTS capital page returned HTTP 503 and its announcement HTTP 403 during this pass. The initial official indexed-table transcription and previously inspected announcement remain the evidence. This pass does not claim a fresh independent verification of those HTML contents. MTS is the weakest source-access part of the release.

The reference corpus is curated with AI assistance. Exact replay can preserve a transcription error as faithfully as a correct fact. The work does not demonstrate autonomous discovery, live registry access, extraction accuracy, complete group reconstruction, legal beneficial-ownership determination or production security. The 2×2 solver and direct-path arithmetic implement only their stated restricted models.

No change to the existing repository license has been made in this review. The paper is prepared for SSRN; submission, author attestations and platform acceptance have not occurred through this workflow.
